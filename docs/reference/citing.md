# Citing binference

If binference contributes to your work, please cite the software using its
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/joshspeagle/binference/blob/main/CITATION.cff)
(GitHub's "Cite this repository" button produces BibTeX and APA from it), and
the method papers that apply to your problem:

- the hierarchical model for binned likelihoods and its Gibbs sampler:
  Leistedt, Mortlock & Peiris (2016), *MNRAS* 460, 4258,
  [doi:10.1093/mnras/stw1304](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw1304);
- population inference from per-object likelihoods or posterior samples with
  interim priors: Hogg, Myers & Bovy (2010), *ApJ* 725, 2166,
  [doi:10.1088/0004-637X/725/2/2166](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/725/2/2166);
- selection effects in population inference: Mandel, Farr & Gair (2019), *MNRAS* 486, 1086;
- the No-U-Turn Sampler: Hoffman & Gelman (2014), *JMLR* 15, 1593;
- rank-normalised R-hat and ESS: Vehtari et al. (2021), *Bayesian Analysis* 16, 667;
- simulation-based calibration: Talts et al. (2018), arXiv:1804.06788;
- Hilbert-space Gaussian processes: Solin & Särkkä (2020), *Statistics and Computing* 30, 419;
- Pareto-smoothed importance sampling: Vehtari et al. (2024), *JMLR* 25, 72.

# Citing binference

If binference contributes to your work, please cite the software using its
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/joshspeagle/binference/blob/main/CITATION.cff)
(GitHub's "Cite this repository" button produces BibTeX and APA from it), and
the method papers that apply to your problem:

- the hierarchical model for binned likelihoods and its Gibbs sampler:
  {cite:t}`leistedt2016`;
- population inference from per-object likelihoods or posterior samples with
  interim priors: {cite:t}`hogg2010`;
- selection effects in population inference: {cite:t}`mandel2019`;
- the No-U-Turn Sampler: {cite:t}`hoffman2014`;
- rank-normalised R-hat and ESS: {cite:t}`vehtari2021`;
- simulation-based calibration: {cite:t}`talts2018`;
- Hilbert-space Gaussian processes: {cite:t}`solin2020`;
- Pareto-smoothed importance sampling: {cite:t}`vehtari2024`.

Their full entries, with DOIs and arXiv identifiers, are in the {doc}`bibliography`.

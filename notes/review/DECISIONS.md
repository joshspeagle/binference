| 62 | 2026-10-25 | C1 | Measures and masks on Bins | change | `Measure` (linear, log, transform with its inverse, density via cumulative trapezoid on 4097 points, tabulated) per grid axis; bin `centers` are midpoints in u (geometric means for log bins); `volumes` stay in z, with `u_edges` alongside; values drawn within a bin are uniform in u.

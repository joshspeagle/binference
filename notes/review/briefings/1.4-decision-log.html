§2

D1–D16

D1. NumPy/SciPy core (+ threadpoolctl); JAX later as an optional backend settled

Why then: Install anywhere; the gradient is two matrix–vector products.

Now: Kept by #3; GPU path and JAX after v1 (#41). Speed at ordinary scales stays a v1 goal (#33).

D2. NUTS is the default engine, in ILR coordinates at its step

Why then: Gibbs mixes poorly with broad PDFs; exact; Stan made the same simplex choice.

Now: Superseded in part: the default engine becomes an adaptive choice from the benchmark matrix (#14, #42). ILR vs tree-matched balance coordinates is parked for 3.1/4.2.

D3. Default prior Dirichlet(1) at its step

Why then: Familiar and robust for samplers.

Now: Spec open question 2 (α = 1 or 1/K). Decide after the Dirichlet-family deep dive (3.1), with #14: the default may itself be adaptive, and α tied to the measure/base measure (#21) keeps re-binning consistent.

D4. Posteriors require an explicit interim prior settled

Why then: Correctness.

Now: Kept and strengthened: shared density class, one of kind/interim required, constructors named by input form (#24, #26, #32).

D5. Distribution name pastry-inference, import name pastry decide now

Why then: pastry is taken on PyPI (a 2018 project).

Now: Spec open question 1. Recommend keeping it: the import name is what users type.

D6. Python ≥ 3.11 settled

Why then: Astronomy users lag SPEC 0.

Now: Replaced: Python ≥ 3.13 for v1 (#41).

D7. Flows deferred to the roadmap settled

Why then: No evidence flows beat grid models for binned inputs; nothing calibrates them.

Now: Kept (#36, #41). B-spline bases and internal resolution (#4, #5) come first as the step towards continuous populations.

D8. Research notes and the landscape report live in the repository (report also in the docs) decide now

Why then: Traceability.

Now: Recommend keeping, with one check before going public: the notes quote and link third-party code and papers, which is fine, but should be reviewed once for anything you'd rather not publish.

D9. Array selections need an explicit selection_mode settled

Why then: The two modes give different likelihoods; the wrong one biases ρ badly.

Now: Kept; renamed on_measurement / on_truth (#7), combined selection (#8), inputs and joint inference (#10, #30).

D10. Laplace: Student-t (df = 5) proposal; flag Pareto k̂ ≥ 0.7 at its step

Why then: Gaussian proposals gave borderline k̂ even for K = 3.

Now: Read in 4.5 (Laplace). Known weakness on the open list; options become exposed (#16).

D11. MAP for tree priors = mode of the density on the simplex at its step

Why then: Matches the Dirichlet convention; unconstrained mode via space='unconstrained'.

Now: Folds into the Jacobian convention (#15) and the choice of best fit returned with every result (#35). Phase 5.

D12. NUTS partial non-centring for logistic priors, dense metric from the Hessian, target 0.9, gate at 0.28 at its step

Why then: 3–10× ESS/s, far fewer divergences for K ≥ 10.

Now: Deep dive in 4.2 (NUTS internals); every constant exposed (#16); evidence from the benchmark matrix (#42).

D13. LogisticGP length-scale prior: inverse gamma, 1% below two bin widths and 1% above the range at its step

Why then: Removes unresolvable length scales; Betancourt's robust-GP advice.

Now: Phase 3.2. Must be restated in the measure coordinate u (#21): on log bins today it is in linear z (audit P-1).

D14. NUTS chains in worker processes via n_jobs, serial by default at its step

Why then: Same random streams either way; process pools have notebook and spawn pitfalls.

Now: Revisit under #33 (fast) and #14 (adaptive): parallel by default when safe? Decide with benchmarks in 4.2/4.6.

D15. MAP uses mix-SQP for Dirichlet priors with α ≥ 1 (with selection: flat prior only) at its step

Why then: 5–10 iterations to a KKT gap of 1e-10, where SQUAREM-EM needs 10⁴+.

Now: Deep dive in 4.4 (SQUAREM and mix-SQP), including the selection restriction and α < 1.

D16. Logistic priors default to a mean of log bin volumes (a flat density) settled

Why then: The penalty acts on the log-density whatever the binning.

Now: Direction kept and generalised: flat in the measure coordinate u (#21). Details in 3.2.

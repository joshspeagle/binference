What the reviews caught

Defects found by the reviews and fixed before merging, each with a test that fails without its fix. A selection:

  • Auto chose Gibbs for jointly inferred responses and selections, and that run failed its own convergence check (R-hat 1.09 and 1.54). It now extends Gibbs until it mixes, or gives way to Laplace or NUTS. #85, found writing tutorials 10 and 11
  • Auto kept Laplace draws whose importance ESS fell short without marking them approximate. #90, found by the vision page's accuracy review
  • attach after a load refused valid results with a basis and a selection, and accepted a model with another prior (prior reprs shorten arrays; priors are now compared by a digest of their arguments). #94
  • PSIS let ties at the cut into the tail, so leave-one-out crashed on resampled Laplace draws. #94
  • Mock posterior samples on unequal bins ignored step widths: with bins [0, 0.1, 3], half the samples fell in the narrow bin. #94
  • The background pages overstated binference four times, for example that a selection on the measurement is inferred from injections (it is a plug-in). #87
  • A full N × K copy of new-object rows under a selection on the truth. #82

Speed, measured on one thread: Laplace's importance step 5.3 s from 28.4 s (N = 10⁵, K = 50); leave-one-out 3.1 s from 7.4 s (N = 2 × 10⁴, 2000 draws); EM one likelihood product per point (estimates identical).

Your calls

Questions parked during the landing, each with the default the code now uses. Work continued on the default; nothing here blocks. The full list is in notes/review/QUESTIONS.md.

  • totals Totals derived under the flat Dirichlet are pulled low when some bins are poorly determined (simulations: mean z of 1.5–1.9).Default: documented in the selection guide. Alternative: a different prior behind derived totals.
  • rates Keep the rates kind as the explicit-rate model (DECISIONS #78)?Default: keep it, and derive rates for every fractions fit.
  • results One result type or two (DECISIONS #85)?Default: two, PopulationResult and PointEstimate, linked by result.best.
  • loo A count downdate for leave-one-out in conjugate fits?Default: PSIS only, with k̂ per object.
  • binning An exact large-N binning-bias calculation and a suggested spacing?Default: the refit check with n_crit per bin.
  • sbc Which uniformity test does SBCResult.passed run?Default: chi-square; the tests require both.
  • speed A float32 storage option, or a compiled kernel (DECISIONS #77)?Default: neither; revisit on many-core or GPU hardware.
  • selection Averaging over selection draws as an opt-in?Default: none; selections from injections are parameters.

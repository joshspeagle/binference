binference: seven directions for a logo

Round 1 of the docs-site design (issue #99). Each direction is a mark, a wordmark lockup, a palette and a type pair, shown light and dark, at 64, 32 and 16 px, and in a site header. Pick one, combine parts, or reject them all; round 2 refines the choice into the full design sheet.

Greyscale checks that each mark still reads without colour.

Histogram b

Bricolage Grotesque · Atkinson Hyperlegible

Why it fits. Bins and the one sample they are built from, in the first letter of the name. On the home page the bins can grow from real draws while the sample settles.

Risk. Literal; the b reads best above 24 px. Teal and amber are common in data tools.

Why it fits. The mathematical home of the problem, drawn as a ternary plot. Quiet, exact, and it scales down to a favicon.
